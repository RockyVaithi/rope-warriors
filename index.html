ROPEWARRIORS

Move. Grapple. Shoot. Survive. Get more kills.

A/D move · W jump · Space or left-click = SHOOT · Right-click hold = ROPE · Shift dash

HOW TO PLAY

The rope is everything. Hold right-click (or E) to fire your rope at the cursor. It only sticks to glowing teal surfaces. Swing with A/D, reel in with W, let out with S. Let go to fly.

Shoot with Space or left-click. Aim roughly at an enemy and the shot locks on for you. Everyone starts with a pistol. Grab yellow crates for a shotgun (huge knockback, also launches you in mid-air) or a rocket launcher (rocket-jump!).

Watch the pit in the middle. Knock enemies into it for the kill — or swing out from under the bridge to escape.

Green cross = health. Lightning = speed boost. Most eliminations after 3 minutes wins. Respawn takes 2 seconds.

Phone: left thumb moves (push up to jump), bottom-right drag aims & fires, top-right drag fires the rope.
